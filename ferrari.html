03 — Scuderia Ferrari

Formula One

Founded by Enzo Ferrari in 1929, Scuderia Ferrari is the only team to have competed in every Formula One World Championship season since 1950. Racing red (Rosso Corsa) was Italy's international racing colour in the early Grand Prix era, and it became Ferrari's signature.

05 — Form follows speed

The Ferrari design philosophy

The evolving silhouette

Long bonnets and rear-set cabins of the 1960s gave way to the wedge-shaped mid-engine stance, and today's cars blend sculpted surfaces with active aerodynamics. Every line serves airflow as much as emotion.

Pininfarina & Italian coachbuilding

Pininfarina shaped most Ferrari road cars from the 1950s onward, a partnership that produced the Dino, Daytona, Testarossa and F40. Since 2010 the in-house Ferrari Design Centre has led design, while Pininfarina remains a historic collaborator.

The Prancing Horse

The Cavallino Rampante was the emblem of Italian WWI fighter ace Francesco Baracca. Enzo Ferrari adopted it, on a canary-yellow shield for Modena, after meeting Baracca's parents. Rosso Corsa completes the identity: red for Italian racing, a century on.
